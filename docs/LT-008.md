# LT-008 Draft Seven-Room World Graph and Entrances

**Project:** Lowtide  
**Deliverable:** A horizontal map showing all seven rooms, their entrances, and the route from the player’s starting point to the lighthouse.

## 1. Room labels

1. **R1 Shallows Tutorial:** The player starts here and learns movement, boat hook combat, and low-tide platforming.
2. **R2 Shallows Hub:** A lamp checkpoint stands near the locked Tide Gate. The player can see the gate on the first visit and follow the open street into R3.
3. **R3 Flooded Street:** Low tide exposes a dry passage to the tide control. Raising the water lifts a floating platform so the player can reach the market entrance.
4. **R4 Sunken Market:** The player crosses a current, collects the Tide Sigil, and opens a shortcut back to R2. The market also contains an optional secret.
5. **R5 Lighthouse Deep Entrance:** Beyond the Tide Gate, the player enters the darker lighthouse district and reaches a lamp checkpoint.
6. **R6 Sunken Bellworks:** The player obtains the Diving Bell, uses it to reach the underwater valves, and opens the submerged passage to R7.
7. **R7 Lighthouse Gate:** The player faces the lighthouse guardian and relights the lighthouse.

## 2. Entrances and connections

| From | To | How the player gets through |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| R1 | R2 | Follow the tutorial exit into the hub. |
| R2 | R3 | Take the open street beside the locked Tide Gate. |
| R3 | R4 | Use both tide states to reach the market entrance. |
| R4 | R2 | Collect the Tide Sigil and open the return shortcut from the market side. |
| R2 | R5 | Use the Tide Sigil to unlock the Tide Gate. |
| R5 | R6 | Follow the lower district passage into the Bellworks. |
| R6 | R7 | Use the Diving Bell to reach the valves and open the lighthouse passage. |

## 3. Map layout

Draw **R1, R2, R5, R6, and R7** from left to right. Place **R3 and R4 above R2** so the market route forms a visible loop back to the hub.

**Player route:** R1 → R2 → R3 → R4 → back to R2 → R5 → R6 → R7

Label the R2 gate **“Requires Tide Sigil”** and the R4 return entrance **“Opens from market side.”** This makes it clear why the player visits R3 and R4 before entering R5.
